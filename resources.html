Resources

Small tools can make a big difference in how you feel.

We share practical wellbeing resources designed to help young people regulate emotions, rest better, and reconnect with themselves in a gentle, realistic way.

Mental health

Mental Health Awareness

To promote mental wellness, reduce stigma, and provide support for individuals struggling with mental health issues.

GBV

GBV Prevention

To educate and empower individuals to prevent gender-based violence, promote healthy relationships, and support survivors.

Addiction

Addiction Support

To provide resources and support for individuals struggling with addiction, and to promote healthy coping mechanisms.

Youth

Boychild and Girlchild Empowerment

To promote equality, challenge societal norms, and empower both boys and girls to reach their full potential.

Community

Support for Elderly and Special Communities

To provide support, resources, and advocacy for the elderly and special communities, promoting inclusivity and social justice.

Care

Education and empowerment

To create spaces where young people and families feel equipped to understand, respond to, and overcome the challenges affecting their wellbeing.
