About us

We are creating safer communities through awareness, empathy, and action.

iCare is a youth-led initiative focused on helping individuals and communities affected by mental health issues, gender-based violence, addiction, and social inequality. Our work centres on youth, the elderly, and special communities, with a strong commitment to dignity, inclusion, and support.
