About us

We are building a safer space for young people to feel seen.

iCare is a youth-led initiative focused on mental wellbeing, honest conversations, and practical support that helps young people navigate life with more ease.

About iCare

iCare is a youth-led initiative that seeks to create a supportive and inclusive community where everyone can thrive.

We are passionate about spreading awareness and promoting positive change on various social issues that affect our society.

Our Vision

To become a leading voice for youth empowerment and social change, creating a world where mental health is prioritized, gender-based violence is eradicated, and everyone has access to equal opportunities and support.

Our Mission

To educate, empower, and support individuals and communities affected by mental health issues, gender-based violence, addiction, and social inequality, with a focus on the youth, elderly, and special communities.

Our Focus Areas

We work across the issues that shape dignity, well-being, and inclusion.

Mental Health Awareness

To promote mental wellness, reduce stigma, and provide support for individuals struggling with mental health issues.

GBV Prevention

To educate and empower individuals to prevent gender-based violence, promote healthy relationships, and support survivors.

Addiction Support

To provide resources and support for individuals struggling with addiction, and to promote healthy coping mechanisms.

Boychild and Girlchild Empowerment

To promote equality, challenge societal norms, and empower both boys and girls to reach their full potential.

Support for the Elderly and Special Communities

To provide support, resources, and advocacy for the elderly and special communities, promoting inclusivity and social justice.

Why it matters

Support should be accessible, inclusive, and rooted in dignity.

Whether someone is learning how to cope, asking for help, or looking for encouragement, we want them to feel reminded that support exists and that they deserve care.
