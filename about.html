About us

We are building a safer space for young people to feel seen.

iCare is a youth-led initiative focused on mental wellbeing, honest conversations, and practical support that helps young people navigate life with more ease.

Our mission

To normalise care, conversation, and healing.

We believe that mental health support should feel accessible, warm, and human. Through Instagram and community-led conversations, we share stories, reminders, and resources that help reduce stigma and create space for real support.

What drives us

We centre young people and their everyday wellbeing.

Compassion

We create conversations that are gentle, non-judgmental, and easy to connect with.

Awareness

We help people recognise stress, burnout, anxiety, and the need for support without shame.

Community

We build supportive spaces where young people can feel less alone and more empowered.

Why it matters

Mental health support should never feel distant or intimidating.

Whether someone is learning how to cope, asking for help, or looking for encouragement, we want them to feel reminded that support exists.
